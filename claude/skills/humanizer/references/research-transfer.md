# Using detection research for a faithful edit

Use this reference when a research-based rewrite or maintenance decision needs
justification. The seven papers study differences between populations of texts.
They do not establish that reversing a feature improves a particular draft.
An editorial transfer needs its own before/after check against meaning and voice.

## Prioritize the actual reader problem

1. **Missing or obscured contribution.** Identify what the author is saying and
   what each sentence adds. Repair generic restatements and scaffolding that
   obscure an existing thought. Keep evidence, qualifications, personal judgement
   and useful emotional or interpersonal work, even when they add no new fact.
2. **Disconnected or repeated progression.** Follow relations between adjacent
   sentences and paragraphs. Preserve real contrast, chronology and uncertainty;
   do not replace formulaic transitions with abrupt jumps or manufactured variety.
3. **Word choice in context.** Use supported specifics and necessary terminology.
   Do not maximize rare words, synonym diversity or a type-token ratio. A short
   specification legitimately repeats its defined terms.
4. **Register and relationship.** Keep the existing person's stance, questions,
   warmth or distance. Empathy, directness, formality and personal narrative are
   choices within the brief, not universally human properties to increase.

This order reflects editorial consequence and preservation risk, not measured
detector weights. Skip an item with no concrete problem. Several observations
of the same repeated framing are one issue, not accumulating evidence of origin.

## What the sources support and what they do not

| Source | Useful question | Transfer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [RelPPR, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33767v1) | Does a claimed statistical explanation actually use its required measurements? | Predictive entropy peaks require model distributions; sentence rhythm cannot substitute. |
| [EchoPrompt, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.05741v1) | Is prefatory material part of the intended answer or extraneous process talk? | A legitimate response is not evidence of latent prompt dependency; this is an editorial analogy. |
| [DivEye, v3 February 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.18880v3) | Are claims about variability confusing different quantities? | Surprisal dynamics and a trained classifier do not prescribe mixed sentence lengths, punctuation or rare words. |
| [Relational Over-Regularization, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26694v1) | Do transitions repeatedly restate the topic instead of advancing this passage? | Generator reversals and structured-human false positives defeat a universal irregularity rule; the editing question is not a computed graph signal. |
| [Linguistic features, June 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04177v1) | Is word choice precise and appropriate to this genre? | Strong group ablations do not establish universal directions; TTR descriptions conflict and effects vary by domain/model. |
| [Online mental-health replies, May 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13245432/) | Does this reply engage with the stated situation and preserve its actual conversational purpose? | English Reddit findings do not justify invented experience, extra questions, exaggerated warmth or general clinical claims. |
| [Psycholinguistic mapping, May 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01800v1) | Do vocabulary, syntax, stance and address fit the existing author and task? | A theoretical mapping supplies no validated causal inference about a writer, feature weights or guaranteed editing benefit. |

## Resolve conflicts and stop proportionately

Give newer evidence provisional priority only after checking that it tests the
same claim under comparable conditions with adequate methods. Predictive entropy,
realized-token surprisal and visible variation are different variables. Retain
conditional findings rather than forcing them into a single more/less-human rule.
The reviewed English data does not establish Russian-language detection accuracy.

After an edit, compare the exact versions for naturalness, voice, coherence and
meaning. Keeping the original is a valid outcome. Distinguish an improvement
over the supplied draft from an improvement over the earlier skill's output.
Council agreement is model judgement, not human-reader validation or proof of
authorship. Add no council to routine edits unless the user or owning workflow
requests one or the existing skill's substantive-tradeoff criterion applies.
