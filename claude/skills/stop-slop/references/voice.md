# Voice matching

Read this reference only when the user asks for a rewrite in their voice or provides representative writing samples.

## Establish the evidence

- Use samples the user identifies as their own or representative of the target voice.
- Follow explicit voice instructions before inferred habits.
- Separate the sample's style from its subject matter, facts, biography, quoted material, and genre conventions.
- When the sample and target use different genres, carry over stable habits without breaking the target genre's requirements.

## Build a working profile

Infer only observable tendencies:

- sentence-length range, cadence, fragments, and repeated openings;
- paragraph length, density, and how ideas move between paragraphs;
- formality, directness, warmth, humor, and degree of visible uncertainty;
- contractions, first- or second-person usage, and domain vocabulary;
- preferred transitions and whether connections are usually explicit or implicit;
- punctuation, typography, parentheticals, questions, and other rhetorical habits;
- recurring phrases or constructions that appear intentional.

Prefer tendencies that recur across the sample. A short sample supports a tentative profile; absence from a short sample is not a ban.

## Apply without caricature

- Match the frequency and function of a habit rather than inserting it everywhere.
- Preserve deliberate quirks, but do not amplify one quirk until it becomes a new formula.
- Do not add opinions, personal memories, sensory details, anecdotes, mistakes, slang, or uncertainty that the target does not contain.
- Do not copy facts, names, claims, examples, metaphors, or quotations from the sample into the target.
- Keep technical terms, legal qualifications, citations, and target-format conventions even when the sample is more casual.

## Validate

Compare the source, sample, and rewrite separately. Confirm that every target claim and qualifier remains intact, the recurring voice tendencies are recognizable without parody, and no sample-only content leaked into the rewrite.
