# Foundation Year teacher key

Keep this file separate when giving the study guide to a learner. It is the original, synthetic key to `foundation-year-study-guide.md`. The sample is provisionally aligned because no institution or official syllabus is named.

| Task | Expected result | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FY-S1 guided check | `150 m / 50 s = 3 m/s` | The cart covers three metres per second on average over the interval. |
| FY-S1 independent check | `1.8 km = 1800 m`; `6 min = 360 s`; `1800 / 360 = 5 m/s` | Dividing 1.8 by 6 gives kilometres per minute and cannot be labelled metres per second. |
| FY-S2 question | `2 m/s` describes recorded intervals only | The table contains no measurement after 30 seconds. |
| FY-S3 source response | “The object covered 20 metres in each 10-second interval from 0 to 30 seconds. The distances at 10 and 20 seconds are 20 and 40 metres, while the table gives no evidence about motion after 30 seconds.” | Accept another claim if it cites two correct rows and states a supported limitation. |
| FY-S4 transfer | `0.9 km = 900 m`; `5 min = 300 s`; `900 / 300 = 3 m/s` | The journey total establishes average speed. Intermediate distances at known times would help detect changes in speed. |

The FY-S4 formative rubric awards four points. One covers conversions, one the division and units, one the interpretation of average speed, and one the limit of a single journey total. An answer giving `3 m/s` alone meets only part of FY-O2. These values were recomputed by division. A different method with equivalent units and reasoning earns credit. This teacher key is a process check, not empirical evidence of learner outcomes.
